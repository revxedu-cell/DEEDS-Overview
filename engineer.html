By the End

Success Criteria

By the end of Engineer, students should demonstrate growth across three core areas of design and professional construction:

1

Design the Solution

Make clear design choices that address a specific root cause, meet defined criteria and constraints, and can be justified with evidence and reasoning.

NGSS ETS1-1, ETS1-2 · CCSS W.2, MP.4 · C3 D4.7
2

Plan & Build

Develop a logical construction sequence, use professional tools and disciplinary methods safely and effectively, and follow the plan while adapting as needed.

NGSS SEP 3, ETS1-3 · CCSS W.4 · C3 D4
3

Test, Revise & Demonstrate Readiness

Test the solution during construction, revise based on feedback and evidence, and demonstrate that it functions as intended and is ready for enactment.

NGSS ETS1-3 · CCSS W.5, SL.4 · C3 D4.6
Your Role in Engineer

Teacher Guidance

Your role shifts from intellectual guide to project manager. Scholars know what problem they're solving and why — your job now is to create the conditions for them to build something real. You set up structures, teach technical skills, manage pacing, and coach teams through the inevitable moments when their designs don't work yet.

  • Work with scholars to define clear criteria and constraints for the solution before they begin building.
  • Identify the technical and professional skills scholars will need to build, and plan explicit instruction for each.
  • Source materials, tools, and any expert or community partners who can support the build.
  • Build a realistic timeline with milestones that include cycles of prototyping, testing, and revision.
  • Prepare scaffolds for planning, collaboration, or construction.
  • Launch the phase with the Design Sprint to help scholars plan before they build.
  • Run the Production Plan daily to keep teams focused and accountable to their criteria and constraints.
  • Circulate during build sessions — use what you observe to target coaching toward teams that are stuck, off-criteria, or avoiding revision.
  • Bring in expert feedback at least once during the build to give scholars an outside perspective on their work.
  • Check that scholars can defend their design choices and demonstrate their solution meets the criteria before moving to Do.
