Phase 2 of DEEDS

Where questions meet evidence.

Students investigate the root causes of the challenge through disciplinary inquiry, building the content knowledge and evidence base needed to propose and defend a solution direction.

By the End

Success Criteria

By the end of Examine, students should demonstrate growth in three areas of disciplinary reasoning and evidence use:

1

Apply Disciplinary Concepts

Accurately apply targeted content standards to explain how the system behind the challenge operates and what causes the problem.

NGSS SEP 2, SEP 6 · CCSS R.1, R.4, W.2 · C3 D2
2

Identify & Analyze Root Causes

Use disciplinary evidence to identify underlying causes, analyze impact and magnitude, and compare competing explanations.

NGSS SEP 4, SEP 6 · CCSS W.1 · C3 D3
3

Evaluate & Justify Solutions

Compare solution pathways using criteria and evidence, then defend a solution direction by synthesizing findings across all instructional cycles.

NGSS SEP 7, ETS1-2 · CCSS W.1, W.9, SL.4 · C3 D4.6
Your Role in Examine

Teacher Guidance

Examine is the longest phase. Your role is to serve as an intellectual guide. In Discover you inspired students to care; in Examine you lead them to think. You design learning experiences, monitor understanding, and challenge students to use disciplinary knowledge and evidence to explain root causes, evaluate competing ideas, and justify a solution direction.

  • Identify the core content standards students need to engage with through this phase and map them to inquiry pathways.
  • Plan how students will generate or refine their inquiry questions at the start of the phase.
  • Curate a range of learning experiences students can draw on — short minilessons, learning stations, text analysis, investigations, research, discussions, expert input.
  • Design or select formative checkpoints to monitor progress across the phase.
  • Prepare differentiated scaffolds for students who need support structuring their research or managing self-directed inquiry.
  • Plan a schedule for short standards-aligned Check for Understanding quizzes throughout the phase. Use results to adjust instruction and pacing.
  • Provide a balance of structure and flexibility, allowing students to take age-appropriate ownership of their learning while coaching them strategically toward mastery of core content and skills.
  • Use formative assessment data and differentiated scaffolds to meet the learning needs of all students.
  • Confer with students individually or in small groups to check that their inquiry is moving toward deep thinking and understanding.
  • Use Evidence Update at the end of each session to help students consolidate and connect what they're learning.
  • Check that students can articulate an evidence-based solution pathway before moving to Engineer.
Check for Understanding Quizzes

Short standards-aligned quizzes administered regularly throughout Examine. They mirror state test formats and give you real-time data to adjust instruction and pacing.
