By the End

Success Criteria

By the end of Do, students should demonstrate growth across three areas of real-world implementation and evidence-based evaluation:

1

Enact a Solution

Implement the solution with real users, systems, or environments, document the process, and adapt when conditions on the ground require it.

NGSS ETS1-3 · CCSS W.2, SL.4 · C3 D4.7
2

Collect & Use Evidence

Gather relevant data about impact and effectiveness using appropriate tools (measurements, observations, surveys) and organize findings clearly.

NGSS SEP 4, SEP 5 · CCSS W.7 · MP.4
3

Evaluate Impact & Effectiveness

Use evidence to explain what worked, what didn't, and why, identifying limitations, tradeoffs, unintended consequences, and meaningful next steps.

NGSS SEP 6, SEP 7 · CCSS W.1, W.9 · C3 D4.6
Your Role in Do

Teacher Guidance

Do is where students put their solution into the world. Your job is to hold the conditions for real implementation: securing access to the community context, supporting students in collecting meaningful evidence, and helping them reflect on what the data means.

  • Confirm logistics for implementation: community access, materials, timing, and any partner coordination.
  • Plan how students will collect evidence of impact during implementation.
  • Prepare the Implementation & Evidence Planning scaffold so students have a clear plan before they begin.
  • Think through what adaptations students might need to make during implementation and how you'll support real-time problem-solving.
  • Launch implementation with clear expectations for how students will observe, collect evidence, and document what happens.
  • Circulate during implementation to coach students in collecting specific, useful evidence rather than general observations.
  • Use Evidence Snapshot at the end of each session to help students capture and reflect on what they're learning.
  • Use the Data Dive after implementation to help students analyze their evidence and make a claim about impact.
  • Check that students can make an evidence-based claim about their solution's impact before moving to Share.
