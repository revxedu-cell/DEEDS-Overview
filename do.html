By the End

Success Criteria

By the end of Do, students should demonstrate growth across three areas of real-world implementation and evidence-based evaluation:

1

Enact a Solution

Implement the solution with real users, systems, or environments; document the process; and adapt when real-world conditions require it.

NGSS ETS1-3 · CCSS W.2, SL.4 · C3 D4.7
2

Collect & Use Evidence

Gather relevant data about impact and effectiveness using appropriate tools — measurements, observations, surveys — and organize findings clearly.

NGSS SEP 4, SEP 5 · CCSS W.7 · MP.4
3

Evaluate Impact & Effectiveness

Use evidence to explain what worked, what didn't, and why — identifying limitations, tradeoffs, unintended consequences, and meaningful next steps.

NGSS SEP 6, SEP 7 · CCSS W.1, W.9 · C3 D4.6

Evidence of Learning

End of Phase

These prompts identify the core thinking students should demonstrate before moving forward. Teachers may adapt the language, scaffolds, and response format while preserving the underlying thinking.

Students are ready to move forward when they can respond to the following:

  1. What happened when the solution was put into action, and what evidence did you collect?
  2. What worked, what did not, and what might explain the results?
  3. What should be improved, continued, or tried next?

Students may respond through discussion, writing, drawing, models, demonstrations, presentations, or other formats appropriate to their age, the learning goals, and the project context.
