Crafting Your Learning Journey

Map what students will learn, do, and produce across all five phases, and make sure each phase sets up the next.

Unit Design

Use the Learning Journey section of the DEEDS Unit Planning Template to map what students will learn, do, and produce in each phase. For a full breakdown of each phase's purpose and pedagogy, visit the phase pages linked throughout.

Overview: What Goes in Each Column
Learning Target(s) One or more "I can... so that..." statements naming the standards or skills students will master, framed within the challenge.
Key Learning Experiences The activities that build student knowledge and connection to the challenge in this phase. Some come from core DEEDS practices and protocols defined on the phase pages; others you'll design around your specific content and challenge. Choose experiences that are engaging, active, and rigorous. Facilitate the investigation and let students do the intellectual work.
Deliverable(s) What students produce to show their learning. A strong deliverable does two things: it demonstrates the phase's success criteria, and it feeds into the final solution. Rather than a stand-alone assignment, it becomes a component of the end product.
Phases
1
Discover Students encounter the challenge and build their case for why it matters.
Learning Target(s) Name what students will know or do by the end of Discover, framed as "I can... so that..."
Key Learning Experiences How will students encounter the challenge firsthand and connect to it emotionally? Jot down initial ideas for simulations, field trips, client interviews, or compelling data that builds empathy and urgency.
Deliverable(s) What will students produce to show they understand the challenge and its impacts? When possible, this deliverable should form a component of the end product (for example, an introductory slide for students' Share presentations, an early draft of a problem definition for a policy proposal, etc.)
Check the throughline How does Discover set students up to investigate the content and skills they will learn in Examine? What core experiences will create the "why" for the learning that follows?
Full guidance on Discover →
2
Examine Students investigate and gather evidence to define a defensible solution direction.
Learning Target(s) These should be "I can" statements that include the standards and skills students need to learn, contextualized within the challenge.
Key Learning Experiences How will students lead their own learning as they investigate the challenge? Consider labs, research, text analysis, mini lessons, or station rotations. Build in formative checkpoints that can be used to gauge student mastery and growth.
Deliverable(s) What will students produce as they apply their learning to the challenge? This deliverable should require students to demonstrate the core content and skills while building toward the solution they will construct in Engineer (examples might include a CER-based argument for the strongest root cause, a research brief narrowing the solution space, an annotated diagram that becomes the starting point for a prototype).
Full guidance on Examine →
3
Engineer Students design, build, and refine a response to the challenge.
Learning Target(s) "I can" statements naming the design skills and content students need to build a solution that addresses a root cause.
Key Learning Experiences How will students develop a plan, build with support from community partners, and exchange feedback across iterations?
Deliverable(s) What will students build? This is the real solution students will carry into Do: a complete product, a finished proposal ready to bring to a partner, or a working system ready to deploy. The solution should demonstrate a high quality standard, something that students will be proud to put into action for clients beyond the classroom.
Full guidance on Engineer →
4
Do Students put their solution into action and evaluate its impact.
Learning Target(s) "I can" statements naming what students need to know and do to implement and evaluate their solution.
Key Learning Experiences How will students implement their solution, collect data, and reflect on its effectiveness?
Deliverable(s) What evidence of impact will students collect and analyze? This evidence should give students something concrete to draw on in Share.
Full guidance on Do →
5
Share Students present their work to a real audience and reflect on their growth.
Learning Target(s) "I can" statements naming the communication skills students need to present and defend their work.
Key Learning Experiences How will students share with a public audience? Decide whether a summative assessment fits your unit.
Deliverable(s) What will students present, and what does it show about their growth across the full journey, not just the final phase?
Full guidance on Share →
