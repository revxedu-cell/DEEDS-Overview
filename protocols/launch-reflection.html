DISCOVER Checkpoint
Launch Reflection
Use these prompts after the initial provocation to help students capture their first thinking about the challenge. Select a grade band to see the prompts.
1
Tell what you notice or what you already know about the problem.
2
Have you ever seen this problem before? Tell one way it connects to your life, school, or neighborhood.
3
Who might this problem affect? Name at least two people or groups and tell how they might feel.
1
Describe the problem and share what you already know about it.
2
How might this problem connect to your life, school, or community? Give one example.
3
Who could be affected? Name at least two groups and explain how it might affect them.
1
Explain the challenge and what you think might be causing it.
2
How might this problem connect to your experiences or community? Describe one connection.
3
Who might be affected? Identify at least two groups and explain how the impact might differ.
1
Explain the challenge and what you already know about its causes or impacts.
2
How might this challenge connect to your life or larger societal issues? Explain one meaningful connection.
3
Who might be affected? Identify at least two stakeholders and describe how impacts may differ across groups.
